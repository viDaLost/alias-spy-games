# Bible geography atlas: landscape gallery

The atlas gallery uses 28 generated landscape illustrations, resized to 1024 × 576 WebP. The original generated PNG files were left intact outside the repository. The illustrations are not documentary photographs and do not reconstruct a biblical-era city, building, clothing, or event. Each place points to the broad regional landscape used for context; a map point marked approximate remains approximate.

The cards link to a public heritage or government page as a geographic or archaeological reference for the region. Those pages are references about the place, not the source of the generated pixels and not a claim that an illustration depicts that exact archaeological site. The illustrations deliberately avoid invented biblical monuments. A regional image cannot settle debated identifications such as the precise crossing point of the Red Sea.

`web/data/bible_geography_gallery.json` assigns every place in `web/data/bible_geography.json` to one of the 28 image regions and carries Russian, English, German, and Spanish labels. `scripts/check-bible-geography-gallery.mjs` checks complete coverage, file signatures, source links, and localized output.
