# V45 test plan

1. Start a level with the pre-level Rainbow Covenant booster and verify one rainbow special appears.
2. Swipe rainbow with a normal symbol without making a match; verify rainbow, swapped symbol and every same-type symbol disappear and one move is consumed.
3. Create/seed a horizontal or vertical staff special, swipe it with any adjacent normal piece without a match, and verify the destination row/column clears.
4. Create/seed a Jericho burst special, swipe it with any adjacent normal piece without a match, and verify the destination 3×3 clears.
5. Verify special-to-special combos still execute.
6. Verify the in-game booster tray contains Sling, Staff, Jericho and Rainbow Covenant; Noah's Ark booster is absent.
7. Buy the Rainbow Covenant in-game booster, choose a clear normal cell, verify it becomes a rainbow special without consuming a move, then swipe it.
8. Verify the ordinary Ark symbol remains available for levels that collect it.
9. Verify hint/no-move logic treats a swipeable special as a legal move.
